## Verdict on the existing local artwork

The production Vercel URL was opened in a real browser; it returned `503 DEPLOYMENT_PAUSED`, so the live composition could not be inspected on 10 October. The existing local build and previously captured desktop/mobile screenshots were inspected. The weakest shot is **Commerce**, where a small seal and circle read as a digital ad overlay, not a material transformation. **Revolution** relies on schematic crowd strokes. The same door, figures and shoe do persist, but all are flat vector signs; institutional claims live mostly in captions. The earlier finale makes an important temporal choice but removes the world in which the claims might act.

## Redesign decision

Keep the shared temporal and interaction system. Replace the diagrammatic room with one materially convincing, consistent architectural image and one identically damaged shoe. Add four physical interventions to that same room: memorial, evidence, monument, commodity. Leave each intervention in place. After the artist's eleven seconds of black and silence, return to the altered room and shoe; end with the artist's new provisional line **NOTHING WAS RECORDED. EVERYTHING WAS DECIDED.** The aim is to make causation visible before text explains it.

All competitor media were viewed for analysis only. No third-party image, frame, document or audio is copied into the artwork.

## Contact-sheet reassessment

The resulting [six-frame contact sheet](artifacts/six-frame-contact-sheet.png) was inspected at full size. The concrete ceiling, tapered piers, red threshold, distant figures and shoe maintain a recognizable camera axis; the memorial, clear evidence box, gesture banners and display vitrine persist into the final room. This materially distinguishes the piece from a quartet of unrelated prompt outputs. The strongest frame is the final accumulation: the doorway is empty while the institutions' interventions remain. Its vulnerability is that the generated shoe and glass box vary slightly between successive edits; the repeated master close-up in the film makes the object's damage more specific, but the artist must still decide whether the visual continuity is convincing enough. The contact sheet also showed that a final close-up alone hid the altered room, so the website and film now return to the whole room before the closing statement.
