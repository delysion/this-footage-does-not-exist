# Competition description (draft)

Eleven seconds are missing from a fictional surveillance archive. Four institutions reconstruct the same interval: a lovers' meeting, a crime, a revolution, a purchasable memory. Their images share a red doorway, two figures and a fallen shoe; their certainty does not share a source. When the original is requested, the archive returns zero frames. An interactive film about how institutions turn absence into evidence, intimacy, history and product.
