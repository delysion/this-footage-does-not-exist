## Film

- `npm run render`: **pass**. [Rendered MP4](../artifacts/eleven-seconds-zero-frames.mp4) is 60.011 seconds, 1600×900, 24 fps, H.264 with AAC audio, 7.8 MB. Metadata checked with the bundled `ffprobe`.
- Extracted and visually inspected frames at 3, 11, 22, 33, 44, 54 and 58 seconds, covering opening, each reconstruction, absence and ending. The four cuts show the same anchors and repeated interval.
- Opened the actual MP4 in a browser video player: **pass**. Browser reported `readyState=4`, `duration=60.011`, `videoWidth=1600`, no media error, and playback time advanced beyond three seconds.
- The soundtrack is generated by `tools_generate_sound.py`; its score occupies the four reconstruction segments. Decoding one second from the MP4 at 20 s gave normalized RMS **0.008342**; one second at 54 s gave **0.0**, confirming an audible signal before the reveal and intentional silence afterward. The score still requires the artist's listening review.
