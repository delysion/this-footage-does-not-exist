## Rejection checks

- **Dashboard/template:** Real browser screenshots show artwork filling the viewport with a minimal transport. Pass locally.
- **Exposition:** The missing eleven seconds and four available reconstructions are visible on opening. Pass locally.
- **Four unrelated events:** The exact architecture, door, gesture and shoe recur. The contact sheet shows cumulative interventions. Pass visually, with minor generated-material drift still possible.
- **Weak reveal:** The requested blackout lasts eleven measured seconds, then the transformed room returns before the verdict. Stronger than the prior error-like zero screen; the artist's emotional assessment remains pending.
- **Screenshot-only quality:** Desktop/tablet/mobile browser QA exercised navigation, sound, pause, keyboard, silence, replay and hard refresh. Production Vercel verification is a separate gate.

## Revision after the artist's October 10 critique

The artist identified a more serious weakness than interface function: repeated architectural shots and still-image camera motion did not produce a distinctly directed human action. The new central shot uses one source pair of reaching arms in all four versions. Romance retains the gesture as a memorial image; Security interrupts it with abrupt crops and a held-subject frame; Revolution freezes and reproduces it; Commerce loops it as an edition. This makes institutional control act directly on an image of a person rather than only adding objects around the shoe. Reduced Motion now preserves automatic narrative progression without moving the camera or gesture.

The remaining vulnerability is explicit: the source hands are AI-generated, and the arm motion is code compositing rather than a photographed performance or articulated finger rig. The person's fate is still conveyed through capture, duplication and repetition of their image rather than through an independently acted aftermath. The artist must judge the updated sequence before it can be claimed as the final competition edit. We must not turn an AI-authored contact sheet into a claim of a physically made set or a fully artist-approved submission.
