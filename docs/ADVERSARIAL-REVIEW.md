## Rejection checks

- **Dashboard/template:** Real browser screenshots show artwork filling the viewport with a minimal transport. Pass locally.
- **Exposition:** The missing eleven seconds and four available reconstructions are visible on opening. Pass locally.
- **Four unrelated events:** The exact architecture, door, gesture and shoe recur. The contact sheet shows cumulative interventions. Pass visually, with minor generated-material drift still possible.
- **Weak reveal:** The requested blackout lasts eleven measured seconds, then the transformed room returns before the verdict. Stronger than the prior error-like zero screen; the artist's emotional assessment remains pending.
- **Screenshot-only quality:** Desktop/tablet/mobile browser QA exercised navigation, sound, pause, keyboard, silence, replay and hard refresh. Production Vercel verification is a separate gate.

The strongest remaining weakness is human sign-off on the actual generated imagery, film and mix. We must not turn an AI-authored contact sheet into a claim of a physically made set or a fully artist-approved submission.
