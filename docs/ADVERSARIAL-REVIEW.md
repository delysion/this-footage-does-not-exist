Internal review by one agent, not an independent juror or a jury score. Public gallery examples were inspected in the earlier audit; no private scores or rankings were available.

## Automatic rejection checks

- **A / Dashboard:** The browser shows one projection frame and sparse controls, with no cards or template interface. Pass in the tested local screenshots.
- **B / Explanation:** `EVENT 011 — 11 SECONDS MISSING` and the four available synthetic views appear on entry. Pass in the local browser.
- **C / Unrelated events:** The same doorway, two figures, shoe, gesture and timecode recur; switching chapters preserves the moment. Pass in the local browser and rendered film frames.
- **D / Weak reveal:** The old instant text wall and shorter AI-timed wait were replaced by the artist's measured eleven seconds of blackness, an advancing clock, actual audio silence, a zero count and the final phrase. Stronger in browser/film; final emotional judgment belongs to the artist.
- **E / Screenshot-only:** Local browser QA exercised navigation, eleven-second reveal, replay, hard refresh, mobile and reduced motion. The revised Vercel Preview and production deployment still need live verification.

The artist has supplied a material final direction, but has not yet viewed and approved the updated MP4, stills and mix. Production testing and this final artist inspection are the remaining gates before presenting the work as submission-ready.
