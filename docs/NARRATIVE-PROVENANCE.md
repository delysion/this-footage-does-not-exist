# Narrative provenance — review note

Within the fiction, the archive has a documented room and a precise eleven-second gap. The missing interval has **zero source frames**. The four versions of what occurred inside it are institutional reconstructions. Nothing in their gestures proves the event happened as any institution claims.

The room before the gap and the later altered room are visible outside that interval. The flowers, evidence case, banners and vitrine are later physical consequences of the reconstructions. The alternate ending's hand reaches for the shoe **after** those interventions, at an unspecified later time. That later action is not presented as recovered footage of the gap. The change in the room, rather than the invented event, is what the ending establishes.

Outside the fiction, all room, shoe and hand imagery is AI-assisted artwork and coded animation. No real surveillance footage, photographed installation or artist-filmed hand exists in the project. The artist has not yet approved the final images or either ending.
