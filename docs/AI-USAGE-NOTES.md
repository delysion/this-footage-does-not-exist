# ELEVEN SECONDS. ZERO FRAMES. — draft AI Usage Notes

The human artist supplied the premise and four institutions; changed the public title; specified the shared red doorway, ambiguous figures, damaged black shoe and timestamp; and personally directed the eleven real seconds of near-black image and silence. After reviewing an earlier version, the artist required a new proposition: fabricated institutional meanings must leave material consequences in the same room. The artist specified memorial, forensic, political and commercial residues, a return to the altered room, and the provisional closing words **NOTHING WAS RECORDED. EVERYTHING WAS DECIDED.** In a later critical review, the artist identified the lack of physical action and insufficiently distinct editing as the leading weakness. They directed a single reaching gesture to be reinterpreted by four edits and requested automatic progression under Reduced Motion. These actual instructions are recorded in [ARTIST_DECISIONS.md](../ARTIST_DECISIONS.md).

Codex, an AI assistant, audited public competition entries, wrote and revised the React/TypeScript/CSS and Remotion code, scripted the film edit, generated a synthetic electronic score, ran local browser/build/video checks, and drafted this documentation. The fictional hall and shoe were generated with OpenAI image generation under Codex's prompts. Six subsequent scene images were created by editing those images in a controlled sequence so the architecture, figures, door and damaged shoe would recur while physical additions accumulated. A further transparent two-arm image was generated with the master room as a reference. The arms were split and moved by code, and the same composite was reframed, held, multiplied or looped in the four versions. This is AI-assisted imagery with authored compositing and timing, not filmed live action or articulated finger animation. Original PNGs are in `assets/source/`; local WebP derivatives are in `public/art/`. There is no physical installation or authentic surveillance record behind these images. No third-party film frame, stock image, licensed recording, external font service, runtime generative model or visitor tracking is included.

The artist later directed an alternate wordless ending in which a hand reaches the shoe but stops at the vitrine glass. Codex implemented that action using the existing AI-generated hand and room assets, with coded movement, a faint reflected fingertip and a glass trace. No artist-supplied photograph or film of the hand was found in the project. The existing written ending is retained as a separate film for comparison.
