# Art audit — 10 October 2026

## What was inspected

- Existing `delysion/this-footage-does-not-exist` repository: React 18, Vite 6, TypeScript, authored SVG/CSS scenes, opt-in Web Audio oscillator, Remotion composition and a locally rendered MP4. Vercel is connected to the private GitHub repository.
- The actual production URL was opened in the in-app browser. It currently returns Vercel's `503 DEPLOYMENT_PAUSED` because publication awaits artist review; it cannot be used to judge the artwork. The authenticated, deployed Vercel Preview was opened and navigated instead.
- Captured browser screenshots at 1440 × 900 of the opening, all four reconstructions and the reveal, plus a 390 × 844 opening view. Examined controls, movement, the shared SVG, typography, responsiveness and the source of the sound treatment. The current browser cannot record loopback audio, so no claim is made about subjective listening quality.
- Read the prize [curator's manifesto](https://conveneo.eu/artprize/blog/human-after-all-the-curators-manifesto) and [judging guidance](https://conveneo.eu/artprize/help/judging). The work must show authorship and a visual language beyond a prompt, with truthful AI Usage Notes and rights provenance.

## Five most consequential shortcomings

1. **Time is asserted, not experienced.** The scene art remains one near-static tableau. Breathing, a color cut and a small overlay do not create a persuasive eleven-second interval or an edited film sequence.
2. **The common evidence is visually fragile.** The shoe is pale and pristine, not the damaged black object specified for continuity. Revolution's crowd obscures the figures and shoe. No shared gesture changes meaning across the four versions.
3. **Institutional authority is carried by captions.** The camera position and geometry barely change; Security adds a reticle and Commerce a seal, but the compositions do not yet make danger, collective history or a sale feel visually inevitable. The single oscillator changes frequency, giving limited sonic differentiation.
4. **The revelation arrives as information.** A zero-frame message appears immediately after the click, accompanied by several explanatory lines. There is no retrieval attempt, time for silence, or removal of the visual grammar the viewer has learned to trust.
5. **The public identity is inconsistent and unfinished.** The old title remains in the website, browser metadata, Remotion film and documents. The production URL is paused, and the artist's final creative review has not yet been recorded.

## Keep / change

Keep the real project, lean static architecture, local assets, absence as an ending, repeated red doorway and accessible navigation. Build one shared, timed physical action around the doorway, two figures, damaged shoe and timecode; make each institution reframe that same action through light, crop, sound and a distinct editing rhythm. Stage the retrieval and negative space before the final two-line statement. Rename consistently to **ELEVEN SECONDS. ZERO FRAMES.** The artist still needs to make and record final editorial choices before a competition entry is represented as their finished work.
